Privacy Policy

This website is a brand and product information site for Prime Pet Family. It does not currently offer account registration, checkout, or direct payment processing.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Overview

What information this site uses.

Website browsing

This static website is designed to provide product, feeding, and educational information. It does not ask visitors to create an account.

Email enquiries

If you contact Prime Pet Family by email, the information you share may be used to respond to your enquiry, suggest products, or discuss retail availability.

No online payment collection

This website does not currently collect payment card details, billing information, or checkout information.

How it works

How enquiries are handled.

When a visitor uses the enquiry feature on this site, the page prepares a message for the visitor's own email application. Information entered into that form is intended to help the visitor contact Prime Pet Family more easily.

Prime Pet Family may keep enquiry emails, message details, and related communication records for customer support, product guidance, retail conversations, and normal business follow-up.

Information should not be sent through this site or email if it includes passwords, payment information, or other highly sensitive personal data.

Visitor rights

Questions, corrections, and updates.

How can I ask about my information?+

Email info@primepetfamily.com with the details of your request.

Can this policy change?+

Yes. Prime Pet Family may update this page as the website, enquiry process, or business operations change.

Where should official product guidance come from?+

Always rely on the current product pack and direct communication with Prime Pet Family for the most current product information.
