Good questions

Before you fill the bowl.

Quick answers for a confident start. For exact quantities and ingredients, always check your chosen pack.

Which Prime recipe is right for my pet?+

Choose the cat or dog range, then match the life-stage and flavour information on the pack. If your pet has a medical condition or special diet, consult your veterinarian first.

How quickly should I change foods?+

A seven-day transition works well for many pets: gradually increase Prime while reducing the previous food. Sensitive pets may need a little longer.

How should I store Prime food?+

Keep the food sealed in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Reseal tightly after opening to help maintain freshness.

Where can I buy Prime?+

Use the enquiry button below and tell us your location and preferred recipe. The Prime Pet Family team can help point you in the right direction.

Can I mix dry food and canned food together?+

Yes, many families use a mixed bowl routine. Just count both foods toward the full day's calories, transition gradually, and make sure the total portion still fits your pet's age, size, and body condition.

When should I ask a veterinarian before changing food?+

Ask first if your pet is very young, pregnant, nursing, elderly, underweight, overweight, chronically ill, or already eating a prescribed therapeutic diet. Food changes are safest when the life stage and health picture are clear.
