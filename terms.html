Terms of Use

These terms explain how the Prime Pet Family website may be used and how visitors should interpret the product, feeding, and educational content published here.

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Using this website

Information first, not checkout or treatment advice.

Brand information site

This website shares product, feeding, and pet-care information about Prime Pet Family. It does not currently process online orders or payments.

Educational content

Knowledge articles are provided to help pet families learn and compare options. They are not a replacement for veterinary diagnosis or treatment advice.

Pack-first guidance

Product packaging remains the final source for ingredient lists, analysis, suitability, storage guidance, and feeding instructions.

Product content

How to read product claims on this site.

Prime Pet Family aims to keep website content clear and useful, but product formulas, packaging claims, bag sizes, can labels, and feeding directions may change over time.

Descriptions on this website summarize the product story and support positioning shown on the supplied packs. They should not be interpreted as guarantees of medical outcomes or as treatment claims.

If there is any difference between a website description and the current bag or can in hand, visitors should follow the current package and contact Prime Pet Family directly for clarification.

Visitor responsibilities

Use the site responsibly.

Can I rely on this site for veterinary treatment decisions?+

No. This website supports product discovery and general pet-care education. Medical concerns, allergies, severe digestive issues, and therapeutic nutrition decisions should be discussed with a veterinarian.

Can content change without notice?+

Yes. Prime Pet Family may revise products, packaging, educational pages, visuals, and legal pages as the brand grows.

Who owns the website content and brand materials?+

Prime Pet Family branding, supplied packaging images, website copy, layouts, and related brand materials remain protected and should not be reused in misleading or unauthorized ways.
